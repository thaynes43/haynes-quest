# Haynes Quest

Haynes Quest is a private browser adventure through the periods of a person's life. Explore for useful equipment, face that era's enemies and boss, then reclaim and absorb memories to grow into the next age and period. Earlier gear and abilities stay with the traveler.

The two-chapter private playtest is complete under [PLAN-005](.agents/plans/completed/005-era-combat-loop.md). Its fictional fixture covers 2020 at age zero and 2024 at age four, ending at age seven. The server owns equipment, health, encounter outcomes, released/consumed memories and age progression. Earlier prototype saves remain readable as preserved albums.

Open the [private playtest](https://haynes-quest-playtest.haynesops.com/) and [visual asset catalog](https://haynes-quest-playtest.haynesops.com/studio/assets/catalog.html) from the home network. Choose **Demo Adventurer** and keep all three fictional pictures to play both chapters. The [playtest guide](https://haynes-quest-playtest.haynesops.com/studio/assets/playtest.html) explains the controls and what remains unfinished; the [handoff](.agents/HANDOFF.md) records the verified release. The original demo address retains the earlier prototype.

The catalog contains 33 entries with thumbnail navigation, inspiration images, 24 available model previews and four sound auditions. It labels the 15 models used in the playtest and separates paused work and rejected enemy studies. The isolated playtest includes candidate artwork for review; final artwork approval, audio listening and physical iPhone/iPad Safari checks remain pending.

The current fixture supplies fictional illustrations and cannot access real photos. Authentik admission and actual Immich player integration remain deferred; the isolated server adapter does not make photos available to the demo. Automatic personal likeness and the full historical campaign remain additional work.

Start with the [project brief](docs/prds/001-project-brief.md) and [documentation index](docs/README.md). The current release, remaining work and durable checkpoints live in [`.agents/HANDOFF.md`](.agents/HANDOFF.md).
