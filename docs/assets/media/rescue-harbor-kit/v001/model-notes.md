# Rescue Harbor kit · production notes

The approved September 29 sheet governs this version. The model preserves the open splayed trestle, X-braces, balcony, doghouse-arch door, bone sign, telescope, hip roof, beacon and horns. The bollard retains its waisted neck, yellow mushroom cap, rope, tide band, barnacles and starfish. The buoy stand retains its red-and-cream ring, grab line, throw rope and blank bone header. The boat retains the shaped hull, waterline stripe, yellow fender, three thwarts, shipped oars, two flank rings, two paw prints and outboard.

Topology is rebuilt at controlled resolution, preserving feature locations. The boat uses 22 hull stations, six section intervals per side, sixteen-segment flank rings and one bow rope coil. The master retains editable parts and exact joined export meshes. Vertex colours carry the palette and gentle baked contact shading; the tower uses matte, satin, gloss and a weak beacon emission material. Other props use matte and satin. No texture, skin, clip, external buffer or decoder is used.

Registry planning boxes remain unchanged. Tight measured bounds are separate evidence. Every A2 instance retains Y = -1.4 m. The open tower and stand do not add a wall; all four props remain decorative with no gameplay collision.
