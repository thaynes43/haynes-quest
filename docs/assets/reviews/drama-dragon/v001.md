# The Drama Dragon

**Recognizable Ender Dragon parody · v001 concept · Model pending**

A tiny crown and theatrical ruff have convinced this angular dragon that the party is his grand performance. Every missed stomp is followed by an offended sulk.

## Intended encounter

The first boss raises both wings before a clumsy stomp. The player has a generous warning and a clear recovery window. Beating him releases the first memory bundle; consuming it opens the next age and its jumping course.

## Construction review

Keep the black angular body, purple eyes, four legs, two wings and one segmented tail. The crown must touch the head and the ruff must close around the back. Overlapping legs in the illustration do not permit missing limbs in the model. Neutral dimensions: 1.80 m high, 2.60 m wingspan and 3.00 m nose to tail.

Minecraft’s Ender Dragon predates the opening 2020 period; the evidence records its 2011 release. The [period evidence](../../../../.agents/work-orders/022-parody-period-evidence.md) records primary sources and distinguishes availability from popularity.
