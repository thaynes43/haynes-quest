## Sizes and placement

The roots are floor-centred, with glTF +Y up and front +Z. Tight measured bounds are recorded separately; the registry keeps its existing planning boxes. All 40 origins stay at Y = −1.4 m. The boat retains its bath-toy placement above the water, with no instance lowering or template edit.

| Prop | Exact width × height × depth (m) | Planning box (m) | Triangles | Materials | GLB bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lookout tower | 3.5000 × 6.8850 × 3.5299 | 3.60 × 7.00 × 3.60 | 4,776 | 4 | 128,256 |
| Pier bollard | 0.5665 × 0.7870 × 0.5665 | 0.60 × 0.80 × 0.60 | 2,632 | 2 | 81,224 |
| Rescue buoy stand | 1.1600 × 1.7980 × 0.3416 | 1.20 × 1.80 × 0.50 | 3,160 | 2 | 80,208 |
| Small rescue boat | 2.2498 × 1.0800 × 5.1667 | 2.40 × 1.10 × 5.20 | 4,732 | 2 | 129,672 |

Every file is below 5,000 triangles, four materials and 1.5 MiB. Opaque vertex-colour PBR finishes carry gentle baked contact shading. The tower adds a restrained beacon glow. There are no textures, external resources, skins, animation clips or compression decoders. These props remain scenery without collision.

The 1.798 m buoy stand remains above the minimum 1.775 m required near the dock. The 0.787 m bollard and 1.080 m boat stay below their 0.80 m and 1.10 m limits. The actual-placement audit checks that nearby decorative tops lie at least 0.5 m below or 1.4 m above the walkable surfaces.
