# Toon Clubhouse kit · v001

**Awaiting Tom's review · used in the family release.** Five static scenery props for the World A chapter 1 `clubhouse` theme from [the locked era roster](../../../designs/026-personal-era-casts.md): a storybook clubhouse tower facade, a curly slide, a gadget toolbox stand, a rounded hedge cluster and a dance stage marker. They share the chapter's cat-captain palette: sea teal, golden ochre, deep plum and warm cream, with moss and lime for the hedges. None of them has mouse ears, paired round lobes, a face, lettering, a logo or a copied clubhouse layout.

## Five exact models

### Clubhouse tower facade {#clubhouse-tower-facade}

A cream tapering tower under one droopy teal cone roof, tilted 10° as in the concept, with a leaning ochre chimney and a cream cap. It has a round ochre cross window, an arched teal plank door with an ochre knob, a broad two-step plum porch and two tucked hedge bushes. The chimney cap tops out at 6.36 m and the roof apex at 6.32 m.

The planning box is only 1.2 m deep, so this is a shallow facade rather than a round tower. The body is an elliptic section 0.208 times as deep as it is wide, and the roof 0.275. Both carry bent normals so the front shades like a round tower. Seen from the side it is a thin slab, and the back is a plain cream wall under the roof. The concept's bushes stick out past the walls; here they are tucked inside the 4.4 m width.

### Curly slide {#curly-slide}

A broad ochre chute on one fat teal post, capped with an ochre ball finial at 3.19 m. The chute starts at 2.10 m, from a small deck at the top of four teal-cushioned cream steps on the right. It half-spirals about 200° around the post (back, left, then front) onto a cream landing at the front right. The concept's S-curve crosses in front of the post twice; the 2.4 m square footprint and the stair on the right rule that out.

## Checks

- **Khronos glTF Validator 2.0.0-dev.3.10** reports zero errors, warnings, infos and hints on all five exact GLBs ([validation](../../media/toon-clubhouse-kit/v001/validation.json)).
- **Budgets and structure.** Each prop is within the 5,000-triangle, four-material and 1.5 MiB prop budget (the largest has 4,772 triangles, three materials and 124 KB). Each has one node named after its registry id with an identity transform, vertex colours and normals on every primitive, and no textures or images. Materials are opaque and non-metallic, with no extensions. Each stands on y = 0 with its footprint centred within 2 cm. The exported scene notes name WO111, `toon-clubhouse-kit`, `v001`, the prop id and this candidate status.
- **Three.js r186** ([author's inspection](../../media/toon-clubhouse-kit/v001/three-inspection.json)) loads every model as a vertex-coloured standard material. It runs the game's own instancing path, which builds two or three batches per prop. At 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° and scale 1 or 1.25, every placement stays inside the registry envelope.
- **Chromium 153** in headless mode with software WebGL rendered every prop with no page, HTTP or GL errors ([browser inspection](../../media/toon-clubhouse-kit/v001/stills/browser-inspection.json)). The stills and beauty renders on this page come from that pass on the exact GLB bytes.
- **Geometry.** No mesh has a zero-area triangle, and the build is deterministic: unchanged props re-export to identical bytes.

The author's [visual notes](../../media/toon-clubhouse-kit/v001/visual-review.json) list the corrections made before delivery:

- The tower was cut from 5,786 to 4,772 triangles, and bent normals smoothed its blotchy front.
- On the stand, the drawer front and tabletop had rendered black where inset cap rings folded. The wrench slot is now a true recess, and a self-intersecting grip band was replaced.
- The marker's squiggle had folded at its peaks, and thin decals had smeared shadow over the top.
- A zero-area cap sliver was removed, and the hedge was recentred by 3.4 cm.

**Catalog intake.** All 98 manifest hashes match the files published here. The intake reran the Three.js inspection against the tightened registry boxes. All 40 placements (five props, four turns, two scales) stay inside, and the boxes now fill each model to within a millimetre ([intake rerun](../../media/toon-clubhouse-kit/v001/intake-three-inspection.json)). A repository test loads the published bytes through the game's real asset loader and decor scene. Each exact model replaces its stand-in inside the registered box at its placement, and a prop whose model is missing keeps its procedural stand-in.

The browser evidence uses software Chromium. Physical iPad and iPhone Safari, device frame time and how the props read beside the course remain owner checks.

## Game use

The five props are registered in the `clubhouse` theme kit with these exact files and checksums. Wherever a level places one, the exact model replaces the procedural stand-in, which remains the fallback if the file fails to load. All four World A templates, `family-world-a@v1` to `@v4`, place 22 hedge clusters in chapter 1. They also place four tower facades as the clubhouse and its tower walls, three toolbox stands and one curly slide, each scaled up (the slide four times). No published level places the dance stage marker yet; it is available to any clubhouse-theme level. Decor has no collision, so the props change nothing about the route.
