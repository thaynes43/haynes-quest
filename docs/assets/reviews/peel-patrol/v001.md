# Peel Patrol

**Recognizable Peely parody · v001 concept · Model pending**

The banana safety marshal takes his job very seriously. Unfortunately, his own trailing peel makes every stern warning end in a slip.

## Intended encounter

A long whistle-and-point warning leads into a clumsy sliding lunge. Step aside, let him wobble, then use the tool you found. His first-period encounter can be avoided without jumping.

## Construction review

Keep the familiar tall banana silhouette, navy cap and vest, boots, badge and one left-belt peel. Use exactly one whistle: the action sheet repeats it, but the model must not. The peel, badge and cap need physical attachments. Final measured height is 1.15 m including the stem and cap.

Fortnite’s original Peely form was introduced in February 2019, before the opening 2020 photo period. The [period evidence](../../../../.agents/work-orders/022-parody-period-evidence.md) records primary sources and distinguishes availability from popularity.

The lead generated and inspected this concept using the built-in image tool. No family images were submitted. This is a selected direction for modeling, not an exported model or deployed enemy. Tom’s exact-version review remains pending.
