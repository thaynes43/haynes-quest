## Sizes and budgets

All roots are on the floor and centred on their footprint, with glTF +Y up and the visible front toward +Z at rotation 0. The measured boxes fit inside the existing registry boxes. The tower stays 2.40 m tall and the fence stays 3.20 m wide, preserving stacking and repeated panels.

| Prop | Exact width × height × depth (m) | Planning box (m) | Triangles | Materials | GLB bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stacking-block tower | 1.3575 × 2.4000 × 1.3575 | 1.40 × 2.40 × 1.40 | 4,344 | 1 | 106,928 |
| Toy bus garage | 3.9723 × 2.1950 × 3.1600 | 4.00 × 2.20 × 3.20 | 4,796 | 2 | 121,300 |
| Crib-rail fence | 3.2000 × 0.9000 × 0.1820 | 3.20 × 0.90 × 0.24 | 4,904 | 1 | 121,288 |
| Giant plush ball | 1.7711 × 1.7640 × 1.7708 | 1.80 × 1.80 × 1.80 | 4,368 | 2 | 115,332 |

Each file is below 5,000 triangles, four materials and 1.5 MiB. White opaque, nonmetallic PBR materials use vertex colours with gentle baked contact shading; the ball's fabric finish is matte. There are no textures, external resources, skins, animation clips, morphs or decoder extensions. The five-view contact sheet is the static equivalent of a motion sheet.
