## Scope and gates

1. Replace the earlier too-young arcade treatment with a darker arcade-casino visual pitch that works for younger and older players. Show Tom the actual enemy image. Record the proposed cast, visual joke, readable attack and possible role without claiming final period eligibility or gameplay approval.
2. The lead preserved v001 and selected the [v002 construction image](../../docs/assets/reviews/rat-casino-ensemble/v002.md) from that feedback. This resolves the pre-model look discussion; exact exported art still requires Tom’s final review.
3. The driving Astra prepares matching construction references. Fresh native Astra agents at max own the live Blender scene one bounded task at a time. Save editable masters, separate GLBs, matching stills and clips appropriate to each approved character. Technical targets begin with DESIGN002's provisional character budget and are measured, not assumed.
4. Compare source and model views. Validate every GLB, orientation, scale, material loading, animation and actual browser rendering. Fix material silhouette/geometry problems before character acceptance. Keep every model studio-only until Tom reviews the exact exported version.
5. Deliver every concept and model in the same PR as its review, inventory entry, derived thumbnail and catalog card. Build strict docs, audit the catalog on desktop and phone, merge green PRs, publish the asset studio and verify live media checksums. Record Blender masters and scene ownership in work orders and handoff.
6. The independent three-prop v001 environment kit is complete under WO097. Next build the central Rat Pit Boss and the rest of the six-character cast as separate reviewable Blender candidates, keeping scene ownership serial. Design new levels with the world builder only after the requested cast is reviewable, so layout and encounter reach can use locked model bounds. Final integration of exact art remains gated on Tom's versioned review, date eligibility, ability-safe encounters and physical device checks.
