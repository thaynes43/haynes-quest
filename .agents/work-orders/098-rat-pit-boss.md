## Model and motion

Build a distinct, original Rat Casino animatronic lead: long rat muzzle and round ears, expressive eyes and asymmetrical whiskers, dark tall top hat, black bow tie, red-plum dealer jacket/waistcoat, aged brass details, segmented worn-metal arms and legs, broad showman stance, dice-topped cane. The rat should read as the iconic attraction host and boss at small mobile third-person scale; silhouette and face take priority over tiny jacket detail. Make it theatrical and spooky without gore or a jump-scare pose. No franchise logo, borrowed mascot face, text or gambling instructions. Use no private media or external downloaded mesh/texture. Name collections, objects, armature, materials and animation clips clearly.

Author in Blender Z-up; export self-contained GLB in glTF/Three.js Y-up, forward -Z, with a stationary floor-centered root and feet at ground level. Aim around 2.0–2.2 m tall for a lead boss. Start from DESIGN002's provisional <=15,000 triangles, two materials and <=1024px textures; if visual readability calls for more materials, measure and explain the exception (earlier exact characters used five). Target <=2 MiB self-contained GLB, one skin with at most four joint influences per vertex, an identity/unit-scale scene root and, if using a texture, one embedded <=1024px atlas. No required glTF extension, external texture or decoder request. Give separate moving head, arms and legs with a simple maintainable rig. Keep cane attached to the correct hand or export a clearly documented fixed prop; no floor intersection. Avoid root-motion tracks because the game owns locomotion.

Provide exactly one working clip for each runtime-required name `idle`, `move`, `attack`, `hit`, `defeat`. Each clip must have positive duration, nonempty tracks bound to nodes below the model root, visible motion, and a sensible resting transition. `idle` and `move` loop; `attack`, `hit` and `defeat` play once. Give attack a readable wind-up and recoverable showman gesture with an exact documented contact point: target a 2.0-second attack with visual contact at 1.25 seconds (fraction 0.625), or record a justified alternative duration/contact fraction. Defeat is a gentle comic slump whose final pose holds; do not embed a fade, hide or root-scale removal, because runtime vanishes after the clip ends. Add optional personality clips only after the required five pass. The visual effects and collision telegraphs will be designed later; this character model alone does not declare an encounter.
