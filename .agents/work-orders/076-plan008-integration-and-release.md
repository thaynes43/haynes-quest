## Root decisions and demonstrated causes

- A route memory becomes `revealed` when recovered, but the renderer formerly kept every revealed keepsake visible until the entire chapter was consumed. New v3 routes hide recovered roots immediately; archived v2 reveal behavior is preserved. The frame accepts contact interaction first and suppresses a coincident attack/secondary notice only when that contact request is accepted. Fable also demonstrated a stale-picture lifecycle bug: replacing a pickup notice canceled its timer but retained its photo. Root now updates notice text and photo as one value, with two regression cases. Failed requests retain the collectible and bounded retry behavior.
- The Help button had no disabled attribute. Its pale styling and missing feedback made an unsuccessful audio start look inert, while the parent `touch-action: none` prevented the intended touch scrolling. The modal can now scroll through actual touch gestures; the high-contrast audition button acknowledges each tap, reports bounded failure and stays available for retry. An attempt counter prevents old asynchronous failures from replacing a newer result.
- A Web Audio context could hang in resume, suspend or cue preparation, especially around interruption. WO071 bounds those waits, coalesces concurrent resume attempts, retires interrupted contexts and permits recovery on the next gesture. A final caller audit also found a touch/pen pointerdown could claim a pending resume before the activating release; both unlock and celebration now wait for touch/pen release, with mouse pointerdown preserved and focused regressions. The original four WAVs, gains and limiter remain. Source start/analyser evidence does not establish sound at physical iPhone speakers.
- Besties range checks used the shared encounter center while their visual effects aimed at a specific actor. Rendering and attacks now share actor anchors. The pair turn toward the player, step into each trick and direct hit reactions to the actor struck. Fable demonstrated that a rejected second press could overwrite the accepted in-flight hit recipient; root now captures that recipient only when the action coordinator accepts the hit, with a delayed-response regression. Warnings select the player's position once and remain fixed for dodging; the prior distant fixed side lane is retained only for archived layouts.
- Victory marked combat inactive before the Besties simulation processed defeat, resetting the actors to idle. Defeat now has priority and the authored defeat finishes before both actors hide. The boss HUD waits for actual encounter activation and shows numeric health during combat. Fable’s stationary-child probe justified two-health contacts in fresh v3 Besties plans, leaving existing frozen plans unchanged. The existing post-boss major-memory gate remains authoritative; this repair adds no physical arena wall or new course.
- A real browser trace found an 11 ms world tap rejected after its release handler was delayed by over 500 ms. Pointer timestamps now determine tap duration, preserving the true long-press/drag/cancel limits. The permanent world-tap pill is removed. Help retains the controls. No new models, sounds, photos, integrations, OAuth or dev-env change is included.
