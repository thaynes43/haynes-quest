## Model and motion

Build a clearly rat-shaped, broad stocky gray-brown animatronic, about 2.0–2.2 m including its battered hat. The silhouette needs a padded belly, thick limbs, long blunt rat muzzle, round ears, segmented tail and oversized feet. Give the face heavy glass eyes, simple mouth plates and slight asymmetry. Use **matte worn felt/velour shell surfaces**, stained and repaired seams, a cheap burgundy vest, plain bow tie, restrained dark exposed endoskeleton at joints, and one detachable dice token prop. Make it look like an old family-venue mascot that is oddly still in a dim room, not an entertainer on a polished concert stage. A neutral A-pose and matched orthographic views should make the 3D resemblance inspectable. Use original markings and geometry, no franchise face/costume/logo, gore or jump-scare pose. At mobile play distance, the rat's ears, muzzle, vest and tail must remain distinct against the dark background.

Author in Blender Z-up; export self-contained GLB in glTF/Three.js Y-up, forward -Z, identity/unit-scale floor-centered root with no root motion. Start from provisional <=15,000 triangles, two materials, one <=1024px embedded atlas and <=2 MiB self-contained GLB. A measured, justified exception is acceptable only if the simpler asset fails silhouette or animation readability. Use one skin with <=4 influences/vertex, maintainable head/arm/leg/tail pivots and no required glTF extension or external resource. Model-space bounds must cover rest and animated states, and feet/prop must not penetrate the floor.

Export exactly one working clip for each runtime-required lowercase name `idle`, `move`, `attack`, `hit`, `defeat`. Each must have positive duration, nonempty tracks resolving below the scene root and visibly move the exported model. `idle` and `move` loop; the other clips play once. The acting should be stiff and mechanical, with subtle motor delay or head drift rather than concert-like flourish. Give attack a long clear wind-up; target 2.0 seconds with contact at 1.25 seconds (fraction .625), or document a justified timing alternative. `defeat` ends in a held, funny mechanical slump; runtime handles disappearance, so no fade/hide/root-scale animation. Do not let the dice prop drift or intersect the suit.
