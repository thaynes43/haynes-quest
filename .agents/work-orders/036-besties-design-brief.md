## Approved encounter routine

Working title: **The Bickering Besties**. The name is a lead proposal, not an approved final title.

The duo compete to make the better obby. One produces slow, clearly signaled obstacles while the other poses and cheers. They swap roles so the player handles one active threat at a time. After the short obstacle sequence, their mistimed high-five leaves both dizzy and exposes a generous attack window. The child's found equipment matters during that window.

Use broad safe lanes, short jumps only when the player already has that ability, and nearby recovery that preserves progress. Do not require the player to fight one actor while the other attacks off camera. Show the upcoming obstacle and safe destination from the ordinary camera. No narrow ledges, simultaneous surprise hits or speedrun requirement.

Their defeat is comic: a theatrical fake breakup, then reconciliation. No death, cruelty or scary punishment. The game releases memories only after the whole duo encounter is won, then consumption advances age normally. The duo shares the encounter gate; two actors do not produce two separate boss rewards.
