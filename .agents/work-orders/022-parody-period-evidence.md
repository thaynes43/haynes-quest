## Fixture dates and method

The current fictional fixture is Demo Adventurer, born `2020-01-01`, with memories dated `2020-07-01` and `2024-01-01`. Those dates represent whole-year ages zero and four respectively; the first photo is six months after birth and the second is the fourth birthday. The dates come from the current [overnight contract](../../docs/designs/009-overnight-contracts.md#current-shared-data-and-http-contract).

The matrix separates three claims that should not be collapsed:

1. **Initial availability** establishes that the reference existed by a photo date.
2. **Period relevance** uses a dated first-party popularity or platform signal.
3. **Fixture implication** applies only those facts to the exact current photo date. An exact evidence floor is conservative; it is not a claim that popularity began that day.

## Source-quality limits

- The sources are first-party publisher/developer/platform records except where explicitly identified as direct creator artifacts. Current view counters were not used to infer when popularity occurred.
- The exact Skibidi Toilet day comes from the creator artifact's publication metadata; YouTube's editorial report independently supports February 2023 but states no day.
- Dress to Impress has an exact official universe-creation timestamp and an official December 2023 breakout record, but no located first-party public-launch timestamp. The commonly repeated November 11 date should remain qualified unless a creator launch post is recovered.
- Fall Guys' Steam and PlayStation dates differ by one day. The later August 4 date avoids moving a cross-platform boundary early based on locale or storefront timestamp behavior.
- Among Us is the main 2020 uncertainty: availability is unambiguous, while the exact start of its summer breakout is not. The August 17 first-party scale point is an evidence floor rather than a reconstructed popularity onset.
